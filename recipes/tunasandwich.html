Tuna Sandwich

Description

A classic tuna sandwich is made by combining canned tuna with mayonnaise, chopped onion, and seasonings to create a creamy salad mixture, then serving it between two slices of bread.

Ingredients

  • 1 can tuna packed in water or oil, well drained
  • 2-3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1-2 tablespoons red onion, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard(optional, but adds great flavor)
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • 2 slices of bread
  • Optional additions: lettuce leaves, tomato slices

Steps

  1. Drain the tuna: Drain the tuna well to prevent a soggy sandwich.
  2. Make the tuna mixture: In a bowl, flake the tuna with a fork and add mayonnaise, onion, and optional Dijon mustard.
  3. Mix and season: Combine ingredients and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Assemble the sandwich: Spread the tuna mixture on a slice of bread(toasted if desired).Add lettuce and tomato if using.
  5. Serve: Top with the second slice of bread, slice, and enjoy.
